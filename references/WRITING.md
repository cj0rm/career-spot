# Grounded professional writing

## Transformation pattern

Weak: "Responsible for monthly reporting".
Better, when verified: "Prepared monthly operational reports for the maintenance team, consolidating incident and performance data.".
With a verified metric: "Prepared 12 monthly operational reports, consolidating maintenance and incident data for a five-person team.".

Use **action + what + scope + credible result**. The result can be qualitative when a measured number is unavailable. Never silently add a specific figure.

## Evidence discipline

- `verified`: explicitly provided or supported by a source CV or user.
- `inferred wording`: stylistic rewrite preserving facts and reasonable meaning.
- `unknown`: request clarification; do not claim it in the deliverable.
- `example only`: show outside the CV and label unverified.

An AI cannot know whether a claim is true merely because it appears in a source CV. Treat it as user-provided, not independently verified; user remains final reviewer.

## Summary

3–4 concise lines maximum for typical industry CVs. State role/field, relevant experience or training, important tools or expertise, and value. For students, emphasize relevant education, projects, training and goals, not fabricated work years.

## Sections

- Contact: name, professional email, phone, city/country, professional link.
- Experience: employer/organization, role, dates, bullet evidence.
- Education: official degree or current program, institution, dates; avoid inventing completed degree.
- Skills: relevant, concrete tools/methods; no unsupported proficiency bars.
- Projects: objective, individual contribution, tools, observed outcome, honest constraints.
- Certifications/publications: include only supplied, verifiable entries.

## Never do

- Invent numbers or arbitrarily round unknown metrics.
- Elevate a course project to full-time employment.
- Call an uncompleted qualification a degree earned.
- Claim collaboration as leadership without evidence.
- Stuff resume with copied job requirements regardless of experience.
- Add a false client, employer, award, licence, publication or employment date.

## Targeting

Follow employer requirements; preserve a factual master CV. Store job-specific copies with descriptive filenames like `Firstname_Lastname_Project_Engineer_2026-11.docx` only if that identifying information was actually supplied.
